# Vendor Risk Policy

## Strategic Vendor Thresholds
Vendors with annual contract value above $500,000 require enhanced due diligence. Critical vendors must maintain documented security controls, business continuity plans, and an annual risk review.

## Risk Categories
Vendor risk is assessed across financial stability, security, regulatory compliance, operational resilience, and concentration risk.

## Approval
High-risk vendors require Risk and Procurement approval before contract execution. Medium-risk vendors require Procurement and business-owner approval.
